# MMM Brief

An AI-powered meeting prep tool for the MMM Q2 Leadership Meeting. Select your role, read personalised briefs for each agenda item, and walk into the room ready.

## Features

- **Personalised briefs** for 5 team members across 4 agenda chapters
- **Expertise & depth controls** — adjust reading level and length per brief
- **Knowledge Center** — ask AI questions scoped to a chapter, all briefs, or all materials
- **Meeting material change tracking** — see what's been updated since last review
- **Simulation Room** — rehearse likely meeting questions before you walk in
- **Stakes Detector** — understand what each decision means for your role
